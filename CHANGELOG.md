# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Node summaries, bounded child previews, and explicit topic drill-down, including a direct Robotics → World Action Models entry.
- Connection inspection shows both endpoint summaries, relationship reasoning, evidence and review status from one authorized snapshot.
- Three addable examples demonstrate WAM, physics and independent algebra content with proposal/review separation.
- Keyword proposals require a shared subject tag (title terms when untagged), reducing generic-text and disclaimer-only matches.
- Latest-selection guards prevent slower inspector requests from replacing a newer selection.

## 0.1.0

- Extensible topics, canonical content nodes and cross-domain research collections.
- SQLite persistence, revisions, audit history, transactional imports and relationship review.
- Keyword classification with optional one-model classify/relate/verify workflow.
- Exact evidence/revision checks and stale relationship handling.
- Bounded Cytoscape views, stable layouts, geometric zoom and explicit layer navigation.
- Synthetic robotics, World Action Models, physics and mathematics scenarios.
- Library, CLI, local HTTP interface and contribution/extension guides.
